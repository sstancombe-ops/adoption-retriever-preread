Example Response · Lead Management
How do I use Lead Management to grow the number of customers?
Overview:

Lead Management in Salesforce helps your sales team track prospects and build a strong pipeline of opportunities. It allows your team to work and qualify leads effectively, turning them into opportunities.1

Steps to Enable Lead Management:
  1. From Setup, enter “Lead Settings” in the Quick Find box, then select Lead Settings.
  2. Turn on Lead Intelligence View and add the button to lead page layouts.
  3. Enable Einstein Activity Capture for enhanced lead insights.
  4. Create and map custom lead fields to account, contact, and opportunity.
  5. Edit the Lead Status picklist and set up lead assignment rules.
  6. Optionally enable Web-to-Lead and connect LinkedIn Lead Gen ads.
Practical Steps to Use Lead Management:
  1. Navigate to the Leads tab in Salesforce.
  2. Use the Lead Intelligence View to analyze and prioritize leads.
  3. Assign leads to team members using assignment rules.
  4. Track lead sources and analyze marketing ROI.
  5. Convert qualified leads into opportunities.1
  6. Use custom lead fields to capture specific information.
Value to Your Organization:
  • Streamlines the lead qualification process, ensuring no leads are dropped.
  • Enhances collaboration between sales and marketing teams.
  • Provides insights into lead sources and marketing effectiveness.
  • Automates lead assignment and follow-up, saving time and effort.
  • Improves data accuracy and consistency during lead conversion.1

Agentforce · Help + Product Docs
Overview:

Lead Management in Salesforce helps businesses efficiently track, qualify, and convert leads into opportunities. It bridges the gap between marketing and sales by providing tools to manage the entire lead lifecycle, from initial contact to conversion.

Steps to Enable Lead Management:
  1. Navigate to Setup in Salesforce.
  2. Search for Lead Settings in the Quick Find box.
  3. Configure default lead settings, including assignment rules and lead status picklists.
  4. Enable Lead Intelligence View for enhanced insights.
  5. Set up Web-to-Lead to capture leads from your website.
  6. If using LinkedIn Lead Gen ads, connect them for automatic lead creation.2

Ready to enable Lead Management for your team?

Practical Steps to Use Lead Management:
  1. Generate Leads: Use Web-to-Lead or LinkedIn Lead Gen ads to capture leads.1
  2. Qualify Leads: Use custom lead fields to track specific information and qualify leads.
  3. Assign Leads: Set up lead assignment rules to route leads to the right sales reps.2
  4. Nurture Leads: Engage leads with personalized communication to build trust.
  5. Convert Leads: Map custom lead fields to account, contact, and opportunity fields.
Value to Your Organization:
  • Streamlines the lead lifecycle, ensuring no lead is overlooked.
  • Enhances collaboration between marketing and sales teams.
  • Provides actionable insights to improve marketing strategies.
  • Increases efficiency with automated lead routing and assignment.
  • Boosts ROI by focusing on high-quality leads.
