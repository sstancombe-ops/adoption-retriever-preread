The Challenge

Why the current model isn't enough

Human Error
Users are accustomed to navigating around it
↔
Agentic Error
A new category users are learning to evaluate

In a world where agent-guided self-service is becoming the norm, humans still long for connectivity to others. The challenge is integrating trusted human insights with agent intelligence to achieve a necessary "balance of intellect."

▼
  • Users have developed a skepticism toward standalone AI, shifting their focus to "agentic error" in a way that mirrors their views on human error
  • Communities (Trailhead, Partners, Developer) contain the most reliable solutions because users rely on the collective expertise of their peers
  • The relationship between AI + humans is not about replacing people, but about finding synergistic balance to drive collaborative success
  • Standalone AI is no longer seen as inherently authoritative — one signal remains consistently trusted: the voice of the community

Executive Summary

AI informs. Community validates. Together, they enable action.

Verify, then Trust → Co-create, then Trust
Bottom Line
Confidence comes from consensus, not computation.
What this means in practice ▼

Trust is no longer a gate at the end of the journey — it is constructed in real time through the interplay of:

  • Agent intelligence: speed, synthesis, execution
  • Human insight: context, nuance, lived experience
  • Community validation: proof, consensus, credibility

The opportunity is not to replace verification, but to embed it into the experience itself — so users no longer have to leave the flow to confirm what they've been told.

  • AI surfaces the right content, at the right moment
  • Community and experts validate what actually works
  • Customers act with confidence, not guesswork

Product point of view — Lifecycle Success

Answer Quality operating model for Adoption Retriever expansion

From the Product seat, Qual answer-quality is not a side checklist — it is the critical path when we roll a new cloud or content source into the shared Adoption Retriever. This view shows who does what, what Product owns end-to-end, and how environment readiness feeds Qual (STG for official ATA — not the path to production) — so eng and business partners see one story, and the complexity of each expansion is visible before we commit a date.

Framed for stakeholder clarity: Answer Quality is the spine. Product and Business Partners own scoping. Product orchestrates the baseline suite (utterances + draft expected answers from approved sources); Evals owns tooling / suite fit / ATA run + insights — not expected-answer authorship. Business Partners / SMEs validate and lock expected answers. Engineering feeds environment readiness into Qual. STG is where we prefer to grade — it is not the production path.

Why lock this now

H2 is expansion after expansion — the process has to outlast any one release or person

01 — Repeatable through December

Cloud / content expansion loop

Sept is the first of several H2 expansions. Without a locked Qual path, every new cloud reinvents utterances, SME review, env, and go/no-go — burning calendar we do not have.

02 — Durable through org change

Quality stays in the process

Evals-side org changes are in flight. The bar and handoffs need to remain clear regardless of who runs ATA or Product day-to-day.

03 — One locked Qual story

Shared story for eng + business partners

Official ATA env (prefer STG) and who owns each handoff must be lockable and shareable — not reopened in every EM or business thread. Env for Qual is separate from path to production.

From Product’s AQ seat: STG is where we prefer to grade. Production cutover is a separate eng track. Do not conflate the two in stakeholder conversations.

What multiplies when we add a cloud or content source

Per expansion, Product must re-complete

  • Utterance set sized to the new cloud / pathway
  • SME-locked expected answers + Best Content Sources (BCS), Product-orchestrated drafts
  • SME bench (right cloud experts) + review window
  • Negative / leakage cases when shared retriever grows
  • Workback tied to env readiness — not hope dates

Cross-team dependencies that move in parallel

  • CSDP: content in shared Adoption Retriever index
  • DS eng: Agent build on the same env as Qual
  • Evals: ATA suite + STG profile / signals

  • Program: one AQ story so EM and business threads do not fork

The Next Phase for Agentic Success

Our maturity path — click each phase for what's in/out of the index and what it unlocks

Foundation

1. Comprehension

Unlocks: Content Understanding
  • Ingest & structure Trailhead, video, and community content
  • Establish a shared knowledge base for the agent
▼
What's in the index:
  • Trailhead modules & units (3,656 mapped)
  • Product Documentation (Sales, Service)
  • Knowledge Articles (active, EN)
Notable exclusions:
  • Videos (Wave 2), Community Q&A (Wave 3)
  • Non-English content
  • Deprecated / archived articles
Phase unlocks: Agent can respond to "how do I set up X?" with structured Trailhead guidance and product docs — single-source, no personalization.
Elevation

2. Conscientious ✔

Unlocks: Trustworthy Guidance
  • Apply quality & compliance guardrails to every recommendation
  • Validate against expert & community best practices
▼
What's added:
  • Trailhead voice & personality layer (consistent tone)
  • Citation requirements for every answer
  • Guardrail: no hallucinated features, no deprecated paths
Notable exclusions:
  • User-specific context (still generic per cloud)
  • Case history awareness
Phase unlocks: Agent answers are credible and verifiable — every claim links back to a source, and the voice feels like a trusted guide rather than a search engine.
Elevation

3. Contextual ✔

Unlocks: Personalized Journeys
  • Tailor guidance to the customer's cloud, role, and goal
  • Sequence the right content at the right moment
▼
What's added:
  • C360 Data Graph signals (SHIPPED 7/23): contact identity, entitlements, sub-200ms retrieval
  • Case history mixes + search behavior patterns
  • Journey-stage awareness (Onboarding vs. Adoption triggers)
Notable exclusions:
  • Proactive outreach (agent-initiated, not customer-initiated)
  • Cross-org pattern learning
  • Predictive churn scoring
Phase unlocks: Answers become personal — the agent knows your product, your stage, and your gaps. Recommendations narrow from "all Sales Cloud content" to "what you haven't set up yet."
Transformation

4. Cognition

Unlocks: Autonomous Action
  • Agent reasons across signals to proactively drive adoption
  • Customers co-create outcomes with the agent, at scale
▼
What's added:
  • Gamification: milestone tracking, adoption streaks, progress badges
  • Journey tracking: proactive next-step suggestions based on completion
  • Cross-signal reasoning: combining usage + case + community patterns
Notable exclusions (current):
  • Autonomous configuration changes
  • Multi-tenant optimization
  • Revenue-attribution modeling
Phase unlocks: The agent anticipates what you need before you ask — it surfaces the next step, celebrates progress, and connects you with peers on similar journeys.
